Mike: the declared protection reference, usually the middle linebacker or the likely blitzer. These pass examples place Mike toward the called slide. Confirm the call; do not change direction silently.

Center in pass protection: an NT on C is the first job. With no NT, C is the middle blitz picker. A 3–4 normally has a nose. The 4–3 example here uses two 3-technique tackles and no nose; a different 4–3 alignment may put a nose back on C.

Run combos: ACE = center + guard; DEUCE = guard + tackle. Both secure the down lineman first. The helper climbs only when the partner controls him. The run examples are teaching fits. 20 goes directly through center; 21 hits the left A-gap between C and LG; 22 hits the right A-gap between C and RG. 23 keeps its inside-zone-left landmark. 24/25 aim outside the tackle, and 26 stretches wider to the edge. These landmarks follow the coach’s instructions.

H = edge help: an additional TE or H-back block is shown in the full 3–4 and 4–3 run looks. This is not a five-blockers-can-block-seven drawing. Assign the remaining formation blocks before using the play. A dangerous backside edge needs a declared answer; the meeting uses CAP or SPLIT. Extra simultaneous blitzers require a protection adjustment or a QB hot answer.
